## AV nodal/junctional arrhythmia
- ![[Pasted image 20261122075005.png]]
	- junctional arrhythmia = AV nodal arrhythmia -> PR segment (and thus PR interval) will be the one affected: prolonged if junctional bradyarrhythmia, shortened if junctional tachyarrhythmia
		- review: electricity in atrium is moderate speed; in AV node very slow; in bundle of His and purkinje fibers very fast.
			- this is because: ![[Pasted image 20261122075230.png]]resting membrane potential in AV node is -60 mV, which is less negative than in purkinje which is -90 mV; meaning there is less drastic "pull" in the AV node of cations (which when influx triggers depolarization); and it's said that -60mV do not activate (or something like that) Na channels but only calcium channels, so in short, AV node depol is calcium-dependent and calcium influx is slower than Na (?) whereas purkinje with its -90 mV RMP will be Na depol so faster.
		- review: PR segment represents depolarization of AV node, but bcs it's very slow, not much is recorded in the ECG thus just straight line.
- junctional bradyarrhythmia are most commonly called junctional blocks = AV blocks
	- review: normal "delay" of depol in AV node is 0.1s
	- ![[Pasted image 20261122080814.png]]
- junctional tachyarrhythmia
	- preexcitation/Wolff Parkinson White (WPW) syndrome ![[Pasted image 20261122081350.png]]due to bundle of Kent (another pathway from atrium to ventricle near after AV node)
	- reentrant ![[Pasted image 20261122081651.png]]
		- if there's an irritable focus (?) somewhere in just one of the two pathways (in this case, let's say near the bundle of Kent), then current will shoot first in the bundle of Kent, and by the time it reaches the area near after AV node, it sees the AV node and stuff around it still excitable (since not yet so and so not yet refractory), and so it spreads there too hence reentrant, and basically on the whole, one current leads to two current, so the heart rate will double e.g. 72 to 144! hence tachycardia. --> AV reentrant tach
		- and there's also if the problem is inside the AV node, thus pathways inside the AV node differ so there's some faster than others, causing reentrant within the node itself ... --> AV nodal reentrant tach ![[Pasted image 20261122082104.png]]
	- now look: vagal maneuvers basically inhibit the AV node (as well?) thus decreasing the current that goes through the many differing AV pathways, and (simply put) only one of the pathways is still carrying current, hence normalizing/eliminating the reentry phenomenon.
## ventricular arrhythmia
- why does it occur? most commonly due to highly irritable focus/i in the ventricle. why can it become so? ^1a6abe
	- ischemia. ![[Pasted image 20261122083147.png]]so, normally, during depol, Na influx comes into cell, and then Na/ATPases will pump Na back out to restore the resting membrane potential. but during ischemia, there is less O2, less ATP, and Na/ATPases will not work as well, thus Na is retained inside even after depol, which would make RMP less negative/fluctuative and potentially triggers too early depol -> hence tachyarrhythmia.
		- if the injury is so bad that the cell is physically injured, the membranes can become leaky and thus Na and Ca can leak in, also triggering depolarizations but not due to current from previous cell/normal firing, but from the leak, thus called ectopic/irritable foci firing. ![[Pasted image 20261122083512.png]]
	- another mechanism is adrenaline. adrenaline will bind to beta 1 receptor, which is a G stimulatory protein -> activates adenylyl cyclase -> convert ATP to cAMP -> cAMP activates protein kinase A -> which phosphorylates calcium channel -> Ca influx -> depol. ![[Pasted image 20261122083804.png]]
## ventricular extrasystole, ventricular tachycardia (VT), V flutter, VF
- when ectopic focus in ventricle causes ectopic firing, it'll be (1) wider QRS due to slower spread since not via purkinje fibers; (2) abnormal shape due to vectors not necessarily following usual directions. since they're "extra" ventricular depols, we call them ventricular extrasystole or premature ventricular contractions (PVC). ![[Pasted image 20261122085205.png]]
- but if the ventricular ectopic firing goes repeatedly and uniformly, it's called ventricular tachycardia.![[Pasted image 20261122085354.png]]
	- and so logically, monomorphic VT would be due to singular ectopic focus. multiple focus would have produced polymorphic VT!
- ![[Pasted image 20261122085608.png]]
	- when there's one or two foci which fires rapidly, 250-350 bpm, that'd be ventricular flutter. but flutter causes suboptimal cardiac output, and eventually the ventricles would be hypoxic, and once many many foci becomes hypoxic they'd fire on their own (see [[kumpulan pertanyaan anestesi#^1a6abe]]), producing ventricular fibrillation.

## drugs in short
![[Pasted image 20261122090806.png]]
![[Pasted image 20261122090858.png]]

- procainamide = class IA
- lidocaine = class Ib antiarrhythmic = Na channel blocker -> good for stopping ventricular arrhythmias
- b-blocker = class II = b adrenoceptor blocker -> good for tachyarrhythmia due to sympathetic response, atrial flutter/fib, AVNRT
- amiodarone = class III = K channel blocker -> prolong repol and = prolong refractory period -> prevents automaticity? -> good for both SVT and VT; primarily for AF/flutter
- CCB nonDHP = class IV
	- -> slows down conduction in Ca dependent tissues e.g. AV and SA nodes hence more effective for atrial compared to ventricular arrhy.
- adenosine = decreases conduction velocity, prolongs refractory, and decreases automaticity in AV node -> hence DoC for SVT (to prevent channeling to ventricles, or to eliminate ventricular response rate to SVT for diagnosing underlying waveform)

- brti prinsipnya, klo pure VT/VF/flutter, kuncinya mem-block Na karena dia Na dependent, thus pilihan obatnya class Ib (lidocaine) sama class III (amiodarone).
	- sementara klo SVT/AVNRT, kuncinya mem-block Ca krn dia Ca dependent atau block beta adrenoseptor, thus pilihannya class II (b blocker), class IV (CCB)
		- dan especially AVNRT, bagusnya adenosine -> tp krn AV juga penting pada SVT utk cegah ventricular response rate, maupun pada VT/VF/flutter untuk eliminate itself if it's response rate dari SVT, jadi dipake juga di semua ini. (oh sm vagal manu jg inhibit AV node makanya sejajar sama obat2 AV)
